About this simulation. The 30,000-sf parcel, 18–21 cottages, $300–$500/sf building costs, site allowance, grant ceiling, operating expenses, and rent caps are teaching assumptions, not verified zoning determinations, contractor bids, funding commitments, HUD rent limits, or market appraisals. The historical December 2025 161 Chama financing scenario informed the concept; this game deliberately uses a different hypothetical site and assumptions. Construction costs shown are for cottage floor area; site and soft costs are added. Real developments also require site-specific surveys, zoning review, utilities, accessible design, taxes, insurance, reserves, underwriting, and market studies.
